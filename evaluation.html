What would you like to discuss?

  • Tooth sensitivity, pain or discomfort
  • Bleeding or swollen gums
  • A broken tooth, missing teeth or an old filling
  • Tooth alignment, your bite or smile appearance
  • A child’s teeth or oral-care habits

Bring any relevant dental records, a list of medicines and your questions to the consultation.
