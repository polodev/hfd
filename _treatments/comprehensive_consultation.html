Your first visit is an opportunity to discuss your concerns, medical and dental history, and goals. The dentist examines your teeth and gums and explains whether further tests or X-rays are needed.

You can discuss treatment alternatives, estimated costs and the likely appointment schedule before deciding how to proceed.
