Root canal treatment may be recommended when the tissue inside a tooth is infected or damaged. Examination and appropriate imaging help determine whether the tooth can be treated.

The clinic’s supplied poster includes single-visit root canal treatment as an option. The number of appointments and whether a crown is needed depend on the individual tooth.
