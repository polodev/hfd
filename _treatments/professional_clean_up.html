Professional cleaning removes plaque, tartar and surface stains that are difficult to manage with brushing alone. Your dentist will assess your gums and recommend an appropriate cleaning and review schedule.

Ask for advice on brushing, cleaning between your teeth and maintaining your oral health between visits.
