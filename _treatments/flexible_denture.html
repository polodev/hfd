A removable partial denture may be an option when some natural teeth remain. Flexible and other denture designs are considered according to your mouth, bite and preferences.

Ask about comfort, cleaning, adjustment visits and the alternatives. Complete dentures can also be discussed when all teeth in an arch are missing.
