Missing or damaged teeth can be assessed for replacement or restoration with a crown, bridge or denture. Your dentist considers the supporting teeth, gums, bite and your preferences.

Zirconia and other tooth-coloured restoration options can be discussed during your consultation, along with maintenance and review visits.
