Bleeding gums, persistent bad breath, swelling or loose teeth are reasons to arrange a dental assessment. Your dentist checks the gums and supporting tissues before discussing the appropriate treatment.

Care may include professional cleaning, advice on daily hygiene and follow-up visits tailored to your gum health.
